The problem

In Singapore, youth and seniors often live in the same estates but rarely talk. It isn't hostility; there's just no shared space. Generic social networks don't help, because they connect people who are already alike.

Genlink is built around the one thing that crosses generations: a shared interest. Users share stories, join events, chat, and even play chess together.

What I built

In a team of five, I owned connections and the home page.

  • Connection requests: send, accept, reject, cancel and remove, all as JSON endpoints so the page updates instantly without reloading.
  • Discovery across generations. Search by name with a filter for youth, adults and seniors. The filter exists so people can deliberately connect across age groups, not just within their own.
  • A personalised home page that shows the interests each user picked at signup and a preview of recent stories from their connections.

Who built what

Teammates built authentication, the storyboard, events, messaging and chess. This page focuses on my part.
